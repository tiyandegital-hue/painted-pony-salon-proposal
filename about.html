One chair.

Painted Pony Salon is Chelsie Martin's one-chair studio in Pinedale, Wyoming — built on a simple idea: you should never feel rushed in the chair.

Every appointment starts with a real consultation. What does your hair do on its own? What does your week actually look like? The cut or color is designed around those answers — not around a trend, and not around an upsell.

It's a small-town salon with a high standard: precision cutting, lived-in color that grows out gracefully, and occasion styling that holds. When you book with Chelsie, you get Chelsie — start to finish, every time.
