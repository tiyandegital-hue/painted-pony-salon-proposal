Terms of service

Last updated: November 2026.

Booking

A booking request is just that — a request. Your appointment is confirmed only when Chelsie replies with a confirmed time. Until then, nothing is held.

Cancellations

Life happens. We ask for 24 hours' notice if you need to move or cancel, so the chair can go to someone else. Repeated no-shows may require prepayment for future bookings — we'll always tell you first.

Pricing

Final pricing is confirmed at your consultation, before any work begins. If your hair needs more time or product than the estimate, you'll hear about it before — not after.

Your hair, honestly

We'll tell you straight if a style won't work with your hair type or your routine, and suggest what will. You can always say no to a recommendation.

Photos

We may ask to photograph finished work for the portfolio. You can always decline — no explanation needed — and we never publish a client's photo without explicit permission.

The website itself

Content on this site (text, layout, concept imagery) is the property of the salon. Don't copy it wholesale; linking and sharing is welcome.
