Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. Written in plain language, because it should be.

What we collect

Only what you give us, and only through the booking request form:

  • Your name and how to reach you (phone or email)
  • The service you want and your preferred time
  • Anything you add in the notes field

We do not use analytics trackers, advertising cookies, or third-party scripts on this site. There is nothing to consent to — and nothing running in the background.

Why we collect it

For one reason: to arrange your appointment. Chelsie reads every request personally and replies to confirm a time.

What we never do

  • We never sell or share your details with anyone.
  • We never add you to a marketing list — there isn't one.
  • We never ask for payment details on this site.

How long we keep it

Booking details are kept only as long as needed to manage your appointment and any follow-up, then deleted on request — just ask.

Your rights

Ask us what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, at any time. Since this is a small, human-run salon, that request goes straight to Chelsie — no ticket queue.

Contact

Painted Pony Salon, Pinedale, Wyoming. Use the booking form and mark it "privacy question" — it will be read.

Proposal note: this concept site has no backend — the demo booking form submits nothing and stores nothing. This policy describes how the live site would operate.
